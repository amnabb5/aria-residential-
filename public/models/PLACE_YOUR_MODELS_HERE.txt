================================================================
  REAL 3D MODELS — DROP YOUR .glb FILES HERE
================================================================

When your real 3D models are ready:

1. Drop your building model here:
     public/models/main-building.glb

2. Drop your apartment models here:
     public/models/apartments/apartment-3.glb
     public/models/apartments/penthouse-12.glb
     ...etc

3. Edit src/config/assets.ts:
     - Set USE_PLACEHOLDER_MODELS = false
     - Add your apartment paths to apartmentModelMap, e.g.:
         '3-A': '/models/apartments/apartment-3.glb',

That's it — no other code changes needed.
See README.md for full details and camera-tweak coordinates.
================================================================
